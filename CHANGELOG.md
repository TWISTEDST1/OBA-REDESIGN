# Changelog

## 1.0.0

### Themes
- **Prism**: graphite surfaces with a signal-amber accent, tactile buttons, tinted
  selections and broadcast tally colours.
- **Prism Light**: the light companion to Prism, with a dark video preview.
- **Cyber Pink**: a neon HUD theme with hot-pink actions, cyan focus and data colours,
  themed radio buttons and on-palette muted audio meters.

### Scripts
- **Show Tools**: session timer, stream health readout, chapter-marker hotkey with a
  YouTube chapter export, and scene-named replay files.
- **Scene Scheduler**: starting-soon countdown with an automatic scene switch and
  optional go-live, plus up to three timed scene switches.
- **Accent Studio**: generate a Prism Custom theme from one accent colour, on Windows,
  macOS and Linux.
